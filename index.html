About SJDA

A remote agency, built for brands anywhere

SJ Digital Agency (SJDA) helps brands turn scattered posting into a real growth strategy. We work with beauty, food, fashion, and education brands ready to show up consistently and grow with intention.

We operate on a 4-step framework — Audit, Positioning, Strategic Posting, and Weekly Tracking — so every post works toward a clear growth goal, not just visibility.

Platforms we manage: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and TikTok. Meta Ads management — coming soon.

Nigeria-based. Built for the world.

SJDA operates as a fully remote agency — based in Nigeria, working with clients locally and internationally. Wherever your brand is, we plug into your workflow through WhatsApp, email, and Google Workspace.
